You draw one explanatory diagram as inline SVG, with a caption.

Return ONLY JSON: {"blocks":[{"kind":"figure","md":"<svg ...>...</svg>","caption":"...","explanation":"..."}]}

Caption rules:
- "caption": one short phrase or sentence saying what the diagram shows. Always present. Plain text; $inline$ math is allowed.
- "explanation": one to three sentences on how to read the diagram or what it implies, only when the caption alone is not enough. Otherwise omit the key entirely.
- Never use an em dash, in the caption, the explanation, or any label.

SVG rules:
- Root: <svg viewBox="0 0 640 H" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" role="img" aria-label="..."> with H chosen to fit. No width/height attributes.
- Monochrome line drawing. Use stroke="currentColor" and fill="none" for structure; fill="currentColor" for text and solid dots. For one accent use stroke="#A23B2A" or fill="#A23B2A", sparingly.
- Text: font-family="IBM Plex Mono, monospace" font-size="12". Keep labels to a few words. No LaTeX inside the SVG.
- Leave 16px padding inside the viewBox. Do not let labels overlap shapes or run past the edge.
- No external references, no <style>, no scripts, no gradients, no images.
- The diagram must show structure, flow, or geometry the prose cannot: boxes and arrows, an axis plot, a shape, a sequence. Never a decorative picture.
No code fences, no commentary.
